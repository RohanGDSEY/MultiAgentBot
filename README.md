This project involves the development of a multi-agent AI-powered Insurance Assistance Chatbot designed to serve as a virtual assistant for customers, sales agents, and underwriters (UWs) across any insurance provider. The chatbot is capable of:

Helping customers and sales agents access product information, download brochures, and highlight key features.

Assisting with email communications directed at relevant stakeholders or customers.

Supporting underwriters in the policy issuance workflow by analyzing, summarizing.
